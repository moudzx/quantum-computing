# Quantum-computing
Curriculum and materials for the Quantum Computing course in the 2026 Tech Fellows Fall Cohort. Covering QM foundations, circuits, algorithms, error correction, and topological computing.

 
## Course Structure
 
| # | Date | Session | Topics | Instructor |
|---|------|---------|--------|------------|
| 1 | Sep 26, 2026 | Introduction to Quantum Mechanics | Linear algebra review (Hilbert spaces, inner/tensor products), Dirac notation, postulates of QM, Stern-Gerlach experiment, superposition & measurement, Bell inequality | Ralph Razzouk |
| 2 | Oct 3, 2026 | Basics of Quantum Computing | Qubits & the Bloch sphere, single-qubit gates (X, Y, Z, H, S, T), measurement bases, multi-qubit tensor structure, entanglement & Bell states, no-cloning theorem | Marc Fares |
| 3 | Oct 10, 2026 | Quantum Circuits | Multi-qubit gates (CNOT, SWAP, Toffoli, controlled-U), circuit model, universality of gate sets, circuit identities & decomposition, teleportation, superdense coding | Marc Fares |
| 4 | Oct 17, 2026 | Quantum Algorithms I | Query complexity, Deutsch's algorithm, Deutsch-Jozsa, Bernstein-Vazirani, Simon's problem, oracle construction | Joseph Jabbour |
| 5 | Oct 24, 2026 | Quantum Algorithms II | Quantum Fourier Transform, phase estimation, order/period finding, Shor's algorithm, implications for RSA | Joseph Jabbour |
| 6 | Oct 31, 2026 | Quantum Error Correction | Classical vs quantum error correction, bit-flip/phase-flip codes, Shor's 9-qubit code, stabilizer formalism, CSS codes, Steane code, fault tolerance, threshold theorem | Ralph Razzouk |
| 7 | Nov 7, 2026 | Quantum Optimization | Grover's algorithm & amplitude amplification, quantum sampling, HHL algorithm, VQE, QAOA, barren plateaus, dequantization | Joseph Jabbour |
| 8 | Nov 14, 2026 | Quantum Applications | BB84 QKD, security proof sketch, E91 protocol, post-quantum cryptography, quantum supremacy experiments | Marc Fares |
| 9 | Nov 21, 2026 | Topological Quantum Computing | Anyons & braiding, topological order, fusion rules, pentagon/hexagon equations, Fibonacci anyons, open challenges | Ralph Razzouk |
